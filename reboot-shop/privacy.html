Reboot Shop · Xeurik Games

Privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026

This page explains what information Reboot Shop: Gadget Repair, a game by Xeurik Games, collects and how it is used. It covers the Android app on Google Play. The version you play in a web browser has its own copy of this policy, linked from the game's Settings.

The short version

Reboot Shop: Gadget Repair collects anonymous gameplay stats (like which gadgets you repair, your grades and how long repairs take) so we can balance and improve the game. We don't ask for your name, email or any account. You can turn the stats off at any time in Pause → Settings → Anonymous stats. The app also shows ads from Google AdMob: see Ads below.

What we collect

  • Gameplay events: when a session starts (and roughly how long since your last visit), each shop day starting and ending (repairs done, money earned, parts used), finishing the tutorial, repair jobs offered, accepted, resumed, set aside and completed (which gadget and fault, your grade, time taken, how many hints and slip-ups, whether you made a diagnosis call and whether it was right, and whether something was found inside), how long it took to find and to fix each fault, power tests (whether they passed, and which try), running out of a part and what you chose to do, parts you buy, which shop tabs you open and what you pick on the main menu, settings you change (like sound or picking tools yourself), which screen you were on when you left or switched away from the game (and how far into a repair), hints, upgrades bought and goals pinned, tips you've read, flips inspected, bought and sold (and the profit), the daily crate (started, finished, reward and streak), contracts taken and finished, repair mastery you earn, first-time milestones (like your first repair), when the 3D view had to recover from a graphics error, when something goes wrong in the game (the error message, where in the game's code it happened, and which safety check stepped in), when a save had to be recovered, and when you choose to watch an optional ad for a reward (and whether it played to the end).
  • Session and device data, collected automatically by our analytics provider: a random ID for your browser, when sessions start and end, browser and device type, and an approximate country based on your IP address.
  • Player counts, sent to our own game server: a random ID kept in your save, a random ID for each visit, a short signal about once a minute while the game is open (so we can count players online and play time), what you're doing in general (in the menus, at the counter, repairing or in the shop), the game version, whether you're on a touch device, how many ads were shown, which shop day you've reached, and an approximate country looked up from your IP address. Your IP address itself is not stored. It also sends simple counts of what happened since the last signal (repairs finished, by gadget type, fault and grade, with the time and hints they took; shop days, upgrades bought, daily crates, flips, contracts and optional ads watched). These are added to one total per day for all players together and aren't kept per player.

We don't collect your name, email address, contacts or precise location.

Why we collect it

To understand how the game is played: where players get stuck, which repairs are too hard or too easy, how long jobs take and whether players come back. We use this only to improve Reboot Shop, not to show you ads or build a profile of you.

Who processes it

Stats are sent to ByteBrew, our analytics provider, which processes them on our behalf. ByteBrew keeps this data for up to 24 months. See ByteBrew's privacy policy.

Player counts go to our own server (Amazon Web Services, Frankfurt) and are only seen by us. Country lookup uses GeoLite2 data created by MaxMind.

Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: now and then between repairs, and when you choose to watch one for a reward. To show and measure ads, Google collects your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which gives an approximate location) and how you interact with the ads, and may use them to personalise the ads you see. Where the law asks for it, you're asked for your consent first; you can change your answer later in the game: open Pause → Settings → Privacy policy and tap Ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's Settings → Google → Ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Storage on your device

The game saves your progress and settings in the app's own storage on your device. If backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may keep a copy of that save with it, so your progress can come back when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. Our analytics provider also stores a random ID in the app's storage so that it can tell returning players from new ones.

Your choices

  • Turn stats off: Pause → Settings → Anonymous stats → Off. Nothing more is sent from then on.
  • Delete your data: uninstall the game, or clear its storage in Android's Settings → Apps → Reboot Shop → Storage, to remove the random ID and your save. To ask us to delete stats already collected, contact us (below).

Children

Reboot Shop doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children. The stats we collect aren't linked to a name, email or account.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
