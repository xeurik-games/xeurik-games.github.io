Spin Rivals · Xeurik Games

Privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026

This page explains what information Spin Rivals, a game by Xeurik Games, collects and how it is used in the Android app on Google Play. Spin Rivals is made for players of all ages, including children.

The short version

  • No accounts. We never ask for your name, email, photos or contacts.
  • The first time you play, the game asks your year of birth. It keeps only whether you are under 16, on your phone, never the year itself.
  • Players under 16 see only child-safe, non-personalised ads, and their phone's advertising ID is not used.
  • We count players anonymously to improve the game. You can turn this off in Settings.

What the app collects

  • Anonymous player counts, sent to our own server: a random ID kept in your save, a random ID for each play session, a short signal about once a minute while the game is open, what you are doing in general (in the menus, or which kind of match), the game version, how many ads were shown, how far you have got in the campaign, and your country, worked out from your IP address. The IP address itself is not stored. Turn this off any time in Settings → Data → Anonymous stats.
  • Online play. Online VS and Ranked use a randomly made-up guest name (never one you type) and your top's build. For Ranked and the Gauntlet leaderboard, our server keeps a random guest ID, that guest name, your rank and results, and your best Gauntlet streaks. Players cannot type messages to each other: the only way to talk is a small set of preset emotes.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone, in the app's own storage. If Android backup is on, Android may include it in your device backup to your Google account.

We don't collect names, email addresses, photos, voice, contacts or precise location, and the app doesn't ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Ads

The game shows ads between some matches, and lets you choose to watch an ad for a bonus. Ads come from Google AdMob, and every ad is limited to content rated for general audiences.

  • Players under 16: ads are requested as child-directed. Google shows only ads that follow its rules for children, never personalised ads or remarketing, and the app does not send the phone's advertising ID.
  • Players 16 and over: Google may use the advertising ID, IP address and device information to show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your choice first, and you can change it any time in Settings → Data → Ad privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

Why we collect it

Player counts and online data are used only to run the game and make it better: how many people play, where they get stuck, and keeping Ranked fair. We never sell information, and we don't build profiles of players.

Who handles it

Our own server, hosted by Amazon Web Services in Frankfurt, Germany, is seen only by us. Country lookup uses GeoLite2 data created by MaxMind. Ads are handled by Google as described above.

Children and parents

Spin Rivals is designed for all ages. For players under 13, we collect only what is described above, which is used to run the game (counting players, online matches and leaderboards). Children cannot share personal information through the game: there are no accounts, no typed names or messages, and no links to social media for players under 16.

Parents can turn off anonymous stats in Settings → Data, delete everything on the phone by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage, and ask us to delete or show them anything our server holds about their child by emailing us (tell us the guest name shown in Online VS if you can).

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: uninstall the app, or clear its storage (Settings → Apps → Spin Rivals → Storage → Clear storage). This deletes your progress and the random IDs for good.
  • On our server: email us and we will delete your player counts, Ranked and leaderboard data.

Changes

If what Spin Rivals collects changes, we will update this page and the date at the top first.
