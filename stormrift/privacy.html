Privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026

This page explains what information Stormrift, a game by Xeurik Games, collects and how it is used. It covers the Android app on Google Play and the version you play in a web browser.

The short version

  • The Android app collects nothing. No account, no ads, no analytics. Your progress stays on your device.
  • The browser version sends anonymous gameplay statistics to our analytics provider, ByteBrew, so we can see what to improve.
  • We never sell your information, and we never ask for your name or email to play.

The Android app

  • Your progress stays on your phone. Your save, your settings and any run in progress are stored on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never sent to us.
  • Backups. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app's data in your device backup to your Google account, so your progress can move to a new phone. Google handles that backup under its own privacy policy, and we cannot see it.
  • No ads, no analytics, no sign-in. The app has no advertising and no analytics, and does not share data with anyone.
  • The internet. The game does not send data to us or anyone else. It only uses the internet when you tap a link, such as our Discord, which opens in your browser.
  • Google Play. Google may give us anonymous, combined crash and performance statistics (Android vitals) from players who allowed this in their device settings. Google collects these under the Google Privacy Policy.

The browser version

  • Your progress is saved in your browser's local storage on your device.
  • Analytics. We use ByteBrew to understand how the game is played: when a play session starts and ends, gameplay events such as runs started, levels reached and items picked, plus your platform, language, approximate country and a random player ID stored in your browser. It does not include your name, your email or anything you type. ByteBrew processes this for us; see the ByteBrew privacy policy.
  • On CrazyGames, the CrazyGames privacy policy covers your CrazyGames account, cloud saves and any ads CrazyGames shows.

Children

Stormrift is not directed at children under 13. The Android app does not collect personal information from anyone, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

  • Android: uninstall the app, or clear its storage (Settings → Apps → Stormrift → Storage → Clear storage). This deletes your progress for good.
  • Browser: clear the site data for the site where you play.
  • Analytics: ByteBrew data is linked only to a random ID, not to you. Email us if you have questions about it or want it deleted.

Changes

If what Stormrift collects ever changes, for example if the app adds ads or analytics, we will update this page and the date at the top first.
